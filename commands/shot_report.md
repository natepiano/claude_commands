description: Report screenshot episode time by method, agent, and project.
---

`$ARGUMENTS` accepts `--since DATE`, `--until DATE`, and `--project NAME`.

Run `python3 ~/.claude/scripts/shot_report/shot_report.py report $ARGUMENTS` and show its output. Dates without a time use America/Los_Angeles. If no saved episode file exists, run `python3 ~/.claude/scripts/shot_report/shot_report.py hourly` once in the background with no polling, then rerun the report. `buildlog hourly` also calls this command. A successful scan in the current UTC hour is skipped; the next hour catches up from saved byte cursors.

`scan` reads `~/.cache/hana-shot/timings.jsonl` for per-view attempts. Use `scan --timings-path PATH` to read a different timing file. A default-root scan also reads Mac Codex transcripts and timing lines over bounded SSH, filtering transcript content on the Mac before transfer. It states whether Mac Claude transcripts were cheap enough to include. The Mac read state is finished, catching up, or unavailable in both the saved status and report. Only a finished read advances Mac coverage, stamped at read start. Coverage names the sources included; a finished read with Mac Claude transcripts out covers Codex transcripts and timings only. An unreachable Mac keeps its last coverage time and saved episodes; later reads resume from received bytes. The scan line states source bytes read.

The report starts with its window and last successful scan time. Times show PDT or PST and the actual offset. One aligned row per method, agent, and project puts 5-minute and 15-minute episode counts, hours, duration quantiles, screenshots, and other calls side by side. The kept-shot table counts exact ordered capture attempts separately from attempts inferred from images. It gives each evidence type its own median and p90 attempts before the first kept shot, with its own episode count. A shot is cited when its whole recorded path appears later in the same transcript in assistant text, a `SendMessage` or `codex_mesh.py --message` notice, a checkpoint notice, or a file the agent wrote. Matching timing records include failed views in the attempt count; older timing lines remain valid.

Below are minutes per image, `/hana_shot` call and image totals, and the newest 20 sessions that shot by hand. The weekly trend shows by-hand and `/hana_shot` agent-hours at the 5-minute split. `changes.json` holds the dated product and measurement changes; the by-change section shows both methods before and after each product change at both splits. It flags sides with fewer than 20 episodes and host windows whose covered sources omit a measured source, even when the Mac read finished. Measurement changes are listed separately and never presented as speed gains.

The invocation section reads saved timing records independently of completed transcripts. It counts successes, failures, legacy successes, all eleven failure reasons, and per-mode and per-crop successful attempts with phase-time medians and their sample counts. `views check` has its own invocation kind and stays outside shot episodes; older lines show unknown kind.
